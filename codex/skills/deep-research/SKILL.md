---
name: deep-research
description: Research across multiple sources and synthesize an evidence-backed report when the user requests substantial research.
---

Establish the question, date/version and evidence needed. Search and actually
read authoritative sources with available Codex web tools. Separate facts,
inferences, disagreements and unknowns; cite direct supporting pages near claims.
For software rely on primary sources. For quant research load quant-trading-research
and account for costs, trial counts and leakage. Do not assume Claude hosted
research or connector tools exist. Delegation needs an approved workflow.
Return the requested report/artifact without unrelated documents or external posts.
